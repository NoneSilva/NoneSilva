# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report privately through GitHub: open the repository's **Security** tab and use
**Report a vulnerability**. This reaches me privately and starts a draft
advisory where we can discuss the issue and prepare a fix.

If you cannot use GitHub, write to <erts.sched@gmail.com>.

Please include what the issue is, how to reproduce it, and which versions you
tested. A minimal reproduction helps more than anything else.

## What to expect

I answer reports myself, so there is no guaranteed response time. I will
confirm receipt, tell you whether I can reproduce the issue, and credit you in
the advisory unless you ask me not to.

This policy applies to the repositories under
[erts-sched](https://github.com/erts-sched) that do not carry their own.
